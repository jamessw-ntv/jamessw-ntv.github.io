Factorio — Endgame Base Layouts

Two tile-exact megabase layouts (a main bus and a city-block train grid) sized from the game's own recipe data. Pick a science-per-minute target, see every block on the map, then tick off the build order.
Drag to pan · scroll or +/− to zoom · tap a block for details · faint grid = 32-tile chunks

Raw resources

Every recipe line

Every block on the map is built from one of these line patterns, so a block's footprint is exact for the pattern: its width is machines × pitch and its height is the line height. One square = one tile.

How to use this

  1. Pick a layout. The main bus is the classic first megabase: one wide trunk of belts with factories peeling off it. City blocks are the next step up: a grid of 100×100 squares ringed by rail, one product per square, trains between them.
  2. Set your science per minute (SPM) and module setup. Every count on every tab follows these.
  3. Open ✅ Build Order and work top to bottom. Each step names the block, where it goes, how many machines, and what belts or trains it needs in and out. Ticks are saved in this browser.
  4. Use 🗺️ Build Map to see where each block sits. Positions are in tiles from the bus origin (the top-left of the bus) or as a grid square (A1 is top-left) for city blocks.

What "endgame" means here

The base game's finish line on Nauvis: all seven science packs, including space science from rocket launches, running continuously for infinite research. Each rocket is 100 rocket parts plus a satellite, and returns 1,000 space science packs.

Assumptions

  • Recipes, machine speeds, module effects and the beacon falloff curve are copied from Factorio's own data files (base mod 2.1.21, wube/factorio-data), not from memory.
  • Belts are express (blue), 45 items/s. Mining drills are electric drills (0.5 ore/s, +10% per mining productivity level). Pumpjacks are counted at 100% yield (10 crude/s).
  • Oil uses advanced oil processing, cracking heavy into light and light into petroleum only as much as needed. Rocket fuel and solid fuel are made in the oil block from light oil.
  • Beacons follow the 2.x rule: each extra beacon adds less (the effect is divided by the square root of the beacon count). 8 beacons with two Speed 3 each give +424% speed.
  • Labs: counted for a 60-second research unit with +250% lab research speed. Labs and the rocket launch animation aren't on the critical path for counts.
  • Power is an estimate: machines at full load, plus beacons (about one per machine in the shared 8-beacon pattern) and mining drills. Labs and inserters are left out.

Space Age

This plan is the base game. With the Space Age expansion the rocket needs 50 parts, space science is made on a space platform instead of from satellites, and the endgame moves to other planets, so it would be a different plan.
