## What the real map changes

- **Crude oil is the scarcest resource on the map.** Minimum pace needs about two-thirds of every oil node in the world, spread over ~3 km west of ★ — D is a pipeline/rail outpost, not a desert neighbour. Balanced also needs the oil-well satellites (Resource Well Pressurizer, from Phase 4); Fast needs more crude than the map gives at 100% — overclock the extractors with Power Shards.
- **Copper and coal fit** around the desert with Miner Mk.3; start on Mk.2 and swap as Leading-Edge Production unlocks Mk.3.
- **Uranium** is rare (5 nodes on the map); F sits on the cluster that covers it.
