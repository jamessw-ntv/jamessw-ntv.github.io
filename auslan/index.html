Press Start camera, then show both hands.

How to hold your hands
  • Where: both hands in front of your chest, elbows relaxed, as if holding a small book.
  • Non-dominant hand (left if you're right-handed): open, fingers slightly spread and pointing up, palm facing you. This is the "page".
  • Dominant hand: make a loose fist with just the index finger out. It comes in from your dominant side and taps the fingertip pad to pad, like a pen touching the page.
  • Height: roughly level with each other; the pointing hand simply reaches across to whichever fingertip it needs.
  • For the camera: prop the phone at chest-to-face height, about an arm's length away, so both hands stay in frame.
This is a general guide, not an expert check. Compare with the official pictures on Auslan Signbank's two-handed alphabet and follow your teacher if they differ.
