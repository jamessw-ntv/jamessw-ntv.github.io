How to hold your hands
  • Where: both hands in front of your chest, at about the same height.
  • Non-dominant hand (left if you're right-handed): open, fingers spread and pointing up, palm facing forward, towards the person you're signing to (and the camera).
  • Dominant hand: a loose fist with the index finger pointing sideways across your body, back of the hand facing forward. It touches the fingertip pad to pad.
  • Tilt to meet: for I, O and U, angle the open hand so the target finger leans towards the pointing finger, as in the photos.
  • For the camera: prop the phone at chest-to-face height, about an arm's length away, so both hands stay in frame.
The photos come from Auslan Signbank's two-handed alphabet and show a right-handed signer facing you. Your teacher has the final say.
